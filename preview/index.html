Straight answers

Questions owners ask.

How do we start?

With a 2-week pilot on 3–5 trucks. Your manager sets a weekly target for each truck, you see the numbers, and then we take on the rest of your fleet.

What does it cost?

A simple percentage of the gross your trucks earn while we dispatch them, set by your fleet size. Your manager gives you the exact number on the first call. It covers dispatching, your manager, broker setup and paperwork, tracking and check calls.

Can you keep our performance score high?

Yes. We keep loads on time, stay on every check call, and file disputes when a load is marked late or rejected unfairly, so one bad load doesn't drag your score down.

Do you cut rates?

No. Every rate con is the real one, for you and for your drivers. If your company cuts rates, we're not the dispatcher for you.

What equipment do you dispatch?

Power only, 53' dry van and reefer.

Who do I talk to?

Your manager: one person who knows your trucks, your drivers and your goals, reachable any hour. You don't have to chase the whole team for an answer.

How big is your team?

Big enough to give every carrier we work with high-quality service around the clock, with one manager who knows your company.

Can you handle safety, maintenance and accounting too?

Yes. Our safety team, fleet team (mechanics on duty 24/7) and accounting team are available as add-ons. Pricing depends on your truck count.

Whose authority do the trucks run under?

Yours. We dispatch for your company under your MC.
